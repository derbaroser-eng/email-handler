The problem

Email handling for automated workflows is broken.

Following up with a vendor, getting approval from a client, handling support escalations. Most teams end up with one of three bad solutions: polling a shared Gmail inbox manually, using a transactional email API that is blind to replies, or building a custom email server and spending weeks on deliverability.

Existing tools are expensive and complex.

You need something that just classifies the reply and gets out of the way. No $50/month subscription, no complex setup.

What it does

Feed it a plain-text email reply. Get back structured classification:

approval

Sender approves or agrees. Action: proceed.

rejection

Sender rejects or declines. Action: stop.

question

Sender asks a question. Action: respond.

escalation

Sender escalates to higher authority. Action: escalate.

noise

Not actionable. Action: ignore.
